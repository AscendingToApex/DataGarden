# ✉️ Cold Email & Job Search Reference Guide

Applying through a portal often isn't enough — a well-crafted **cold email** can get you in front of the right person. This guide covers **who to email**, **how to find their address**, **8 tips** for effective outreach, **timing**, and reusable **templates**.

> A cold email's job is small and specific: get a human to notice you and start a conversation. Keep it short, relevant, and easy to say yes to.

## 🎯 Who to Email

Match the recipient to the company size:

| Company size | Email… |
|--------------|--------|
| **< 50 employees** | CEO or CTO |
| **50–250 employees** | Technical recruiter, recruiter, or hiring manager |
| **> 250 employees** | A dedicated recruiter for data scientists (data recruiter) |

---

## 🔍 Finding Their Email

- **Clearbit Connect** or **Hunter.io** to look up addresses.
- Cross-check on LinkedIn and the company's team/about page.

> Note: Guess-and-verify with a common pattern (`first.last@company.com`) and a verification tool if a lookup fails.

## ✅ 8 Tips for Effective Cold Emails

1. **Keep it short** — 50–125 words.
2. **Mention an accomplishment** or two.
3. **Add urgency** and establish a timeline.
4. **Relate personally** to the recruiter or company (reference real research you did).
5. **Have a specific ask** — e.g., *"I'd like to start the interview process for the Data Scientist position."*
6. **Write a strong subject line**, e.g.:
   - *Former Google & Microsoft Intern Interested in FT @ X*
   - *Computer Vision Ph.D. Interested in Waymo*
   - *Kaggle Champion Interested in Airbnb DS*
7. **Follow up 3 times** (see schedule below).
8. **Send at the right time** — the recruiter's local time zone, **Tuesday–Thursday at 11 AM**.

## ⏰ Timing & Follow-Ups

| Email | When |
|-------|------|
| 1st | Thursday, 11 AM |
| 2nd | 4 days later (Tuesday, 11 AM) |
| 3rd | 7 days after the 2nd (Tuesday, 11 AM) |

> Pro Tip: Persistence is normal and expected — three polite follow-ups is professional, not pushy. Space them out and keep each one short.

## 📧 Email Templates

Replace the `[bracketed]` placeholders. Keep your accomplishments concrete and your ask clear.

**First cold email**
> **Subject:** Introduction for Data Scientist Position at [Company Name]
>
> Hi [Hiring Manager's Name],
>
> I'm very interested in the [Position] role at [Company Name]. I applied on [Application Site] but figured I'd also reach out directly.
>
> I'm an upcoming MS in Data Science graduate with a track record in data-driven, results-focused problem solving. I've attached my resume and would love to talk about why I'm excited about [Company Name].
>
> Best regards,
> [Your Name]

**Second email (follow-up, ~4 days later)**
> **Subject:** Follow-up for Data Scientist Position at [Company Name]
>
> Hi [Hiring Manager's Name],
>
> I wanted to follow up on my previous email and reaffirm my interest in the [Position] role at [Company Name]. I believe my skills and experience align well with the requirements. If you need any additional information or would like to discuss my qualifications, I'm happy to help.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your Name]

**Third email (final follow-up, ~7 days later)**
> **Subject:** Final Follow-up for Data Scientist Position at [Company Name]
>
> Hi [Hiring Manager's Name],
>
> Reaching out one last time regarding the [Position] role at [Company Name] — I'm still very interested and believe I'd be a strong fit. What do next steps look like?
>
> Best regards,
> [Your Name]

## 📚 Glossary

| Term | Definition | When it matters |
|------|------------|-----------------|
| Cold email | Unsolicited outreach to a hiring contact | Getting noticed beyond the portal |
| Hiring manager | The person the role reports into | Best contact at mid-size firms |
| Email finder | Tool like Hunter.io / Clearbit Connect | Locating addresses |
| Specific ask | The one clear action you want | Making it easy to say yes |

---

🧠 **Pro Tips:**
* Lead with **relevance and a concrete win**, not your life story.
* Make the **ask unmistakable** — one sentence, one action.
* Send in the recipient's **time zone**, and follow up without apology.
